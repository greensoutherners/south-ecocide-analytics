GREEN SOUTHERNERS

Investigations of the 2026 war

Open investigations into the environmental and civilian harm of the 2026 war in South Lebanon, built from satellite observation and open sources. They count records from 1 March 2026 and read the ground on dated pictures, some of them taken before the war. Each draws its maps from the published data, gives its figures as of the day it was built, and states what its evidence does not establish as plainly as what it does.

The village through 2026

The centre of the village on every sharp picture held of it: before the war, then the four OnGeo pictures of 2026. Most of the buildings of the centre that readers found destroyed or damaged were first seen gone on the pictures of 19 August and 8 October 2026, weeks in which Lebanese media reported the Israeli army detonating houses there again and again. On the picture of 19 August, plumes of smoke or dust stand over the middle of the village.

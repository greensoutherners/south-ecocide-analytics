The investigation, step by step.

1. The castle above the Litani

Beaufort Castle, Qalaat al-Shqif, stands on a cliff above the gorge of the Litani River, between the towns of Arnoun and Yohmor in the district of Nabatieh. The Israeli army held it from 1982 to 2000.

This investigation reads the ground within 5.5 km of the castle: the 13 places of the gazetteer whose points fall inside that circle, among them Arnoun, Yohmor, Deir Mimas, Burj al-Mulouk, Al-Qlaiaah and Kfar Tebnit.

2. What the corpus holds around it

Within the circle the corpus holds 79 fires, 50 white phosphorus attacks and 17 displacement orders. Each kind was collected and is counted apart, so the counts are not added together.

Displacement orders name a town, so they shade the whole municipality; fires and white phosphorus are counted by town, each mark growing with the records there.

3. Fire on the slopes

79 fires linked to an Israeli attack burned at these places between 10 March 2026 and 4 October 2026, the most in July 2026 (22).

By what lit them: 54 by an explosion, 16 with no attack linked to them, 6 by white phosphorus, 2 by an incendiary munition and 1 by illumination flares. Where a burn scar was measured on Sentinel-2, 50 of them cover 1,081.0 ha between them.

4. White phosphorus over the valley

50 white phosphorus attacks are recorded at these places, from 3 March 2026 to 8 October 2026.

16 were geolocated, placed on the ground from footage, a satellite detection or a burn scar, and 34 are known by their village. On the map each town's diamond grows with the attacks recorded there.

5. Yohmor, read building by building

The village damage report read Yohmor, with the castle and the ground within 1.5 km of the town, on sharp satellite pictures.

It mapped 245 buildings destroyed and 30 damaged.

6. Zawtar El Sharqiyeh, on the river

Zawtar El Sharqiyeh lies on the Litani below the castle.

Its damage report saw 262 buildings change on Sentinel-2 and 1 damaged on sharp pictures.

The night of 31 July round the castle

The land of three towns meets at the castle: Arnoun to the north, Yohmor to the west and Kfar Tebnit beyond them. On the night of 31 July 2026 the National News Agency, carried by NBN and Al-Akhbar, reported very large detonations at midnight in Yohmor al-Shqif, Arnoun and Kfar Tebnit, heard across the south and as far as Iqlim al-Kharroub and Khaldeh. Al-Mayadeen's correspondent placed detonations on the edge of Deir Mimas towards Beaufort Castle and between Arnoun and Yohmor. Al-Manar published video of detonations round the castle, and Al-Arabiya and Al-Hadath published pictures of their effects round it. Al-Araby TV reported an Israeli announcement that tunnels at the castle had been blown up with 700 tonnes of explosives.

The sources go on to report a large detonation in Yohmor on 24 August whose echo was heard across the south, more in Yohmor on 25 and 26 August, and on 17 and 20 September, the last also at Arnoun.

The castle, July 2023 and September 2026

The castle stands on the edge of a cliff above the Litani gorge, its stone walls set on the rock. Detonations of the size reported on 31 July put a building like it under blast, shaking and falling rock. The corpus holds no survey of the castle made on the ground, so what they did to its walls is not established here.

Reading rules that apply to every figure on these pages
  • No figure is added across kinds of record. Each kind was looked for separately, over its own window and from its own sources, so a combined total would measure the collection rather than the war.
  • A record is not an event count. Records proven to describe the same event are folded together, but a record stays a unit of documentation.
  • Figures, not records. The maps and charts count records by town and week; a mark sits at its town and grows with the records there. The records themselves are not published: the database shows their figures. To access the records, contact Green Southerners.
  • Everything here is the 2026 war, from 1 March 2026. The wildlife and trees map, where it is shown, is earlier context recorded in 2023 and 2024, and says so.
  • Fires: every fire linked to an Israeli military attack (an attack documented at the same town on a day of the fire, or a source that names it), and every fire in the yellow-line zone or round Beaufort Castle, whatever lit it.
  • Absence is never published as a finding. An unshaded place carries no record of that kind, which is not evidence that nothing happened there.
